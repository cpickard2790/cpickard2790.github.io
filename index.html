Support

Help & support

Answers to common questions, plus help, data details and privacy links for each app.

How do I restore Pro on a new phone?

Open the app’s Settings or Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

How do I get a refund?

All purchases go through Apple, so refunds do too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. I can’t issue refunds directly.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. Some apps offer optional iCloud sync or backup to your own iCloud account, and a few connect to services you choose (like Google Drive). Each app’s details are below and in the privacy policy.

Can I suggest a feature?

Yes, please do. Email me with the app name and what you’d like it to do. I read every message.

How do I restore Pro on a new device?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How exact are the results?

Math is done in exact fractions. When a result is shown at your chosen fraction (for example 1/16″), the app tells you how much it rounded. Change the smallest fraction in Settings.

Can I rely on the stair and rafter results?

FieldFraction is a calculation aid. Always check your measurements and your local building code before you cut or build.
