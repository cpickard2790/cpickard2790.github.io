Bills Due Tracker

Bill reminders, planned by paycheck. No bank link, no account, no subscription.

Bills Due Tracker is an iPhone app for keeping track of recurring bills like rent, utilities, phone, insurance, loans, credit cards and subscriptions. You add each bill once. The app reminds you before it's due, shows which paycheck has to cover it, and keeps a history of what you've paid.

  • Get it on the App Store
  • Privacy policy
  • Contact: support@chadpickard.app

Quick facts

  • Made for: people who pay bills by hand or by autopay and want to know what's due before each payday, without connecting a bank.
  • Works on: iPhone and iPad, iOS 26.2 or later.
  • Price: free to download. Pro is a one-time purchase ($9.99 in the US; prices vary by country). There is no subscription.
  • Free version: up to 12 bills, reminders, the calendar, Paycheck Plan, payment history, widgets and CSV export. It shows small ads.
  • Pro adds: unlimited bills, Scan a Bill, unlimited custom categories, the full Paycheck Plan (several income sources, 3 to 12 pay periods), debt payoff tracking, several reminders per bill, full CSV export with payment history, and no ads.
  • Privacy: no account and no bank login. Your bills are stored on your iPhone. Scan a Bill reads the bill on your iPhone; the photo isn't saved or sent anywhere.

How it's different

  • Planned by paycheck, not just by date. Paycheck Plan groups bills by pay period (weekly, every 2 weeks, twice a month or monthly), so you can see what this paycheck has to cover and what's left after bills.
  • No bank connection. Many budgeting apps ask for your bank login. Bills Due Tracker never does. You enter bills yourself, which takes about a minute each.
  • Pay once. Many bill and budget apps charge monthly. Bills Due Tracker Pro is a single purchase.

What it does

  • Overdue, due today and the next 7 days on one screen.
  • Calendar with monthly totals, and a monthly plan: total due, paid and left.
  • Reminders before each due date, plus an optional daily summary.
  • Swipe to mark a bill paid, snooze it, archive it or copy it.
  • Autopay tracking for bills that pay themselves.
  • Subscriptions view with your monthly and yearly total.
  • Payment history with a 6-month chart and top categories.
  • Your own categories with a name, color and icon.
  • Home Screen widgets.
  • Scan a Bill (Pro): take a photo of a paper bill or pick a screenshot, and the amount, due date and biller are filled in for you to check.
  • Debt payoff tracking (Pro): balances, progress and credit card use.
  • CSV export for backups or spreadsheets.

Questions

Do I have to link my bank account?

No. Bills Due Tracker never asks for a bank login. You type in each bill (or scan it with Pro) and mark it paid when you pay it.

Is it a subscription?

No. The app is free to download, and Pro is a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks everything for good.

What's the difference between free and Pro?

Free covers up to 12 bills with reminders, the calendar, Paycheck Plan, history, widgets and CSV export, with small ads. Pro removes the limit and the ads and adds Scan a Bill, unlimited categories, the full Paycheck Plan, debt payoff tracking, several reminders per bill and full CSV export.

Can it show which bills come out of each paycheck?

Yes. Set how often you're paid in Paycheck Plan and it groups your bills by pay period, with the amount left after bills. Pro adds more than one income source and up to 12 pay periods ahead.

Does it work for bills that change every month, like electric or water?

Yes. Set the bill's amount as variable and enter an estimate. When you mark it paid, you enter what you actually paid, and that goes into your payment history. With Pro, Scan a Bill reads the amount from the paper bill or a screenshot.

Where is my data stored? Does it sync?

Your bills stay on your iPhone and are included in your normal iPhone backup. There is no account and no cloud sync between devices. Use CSV export to keep a copy or open your bills in a spreadsheet.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check Settings > Notifications > Bills Due Tracker and make sure notifications are allowed. Focus modes can also hide them. Then open the app once so it can schedule upcoming reminders again.

I bought Pro but it isn't showing.

Open the Pro screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. If that doesn't work, email support@chadpickard.app.
