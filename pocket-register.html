Pocket Register

A checkbook register for iPhone. Running balance, recurring entries, payday planning. No bank link.

Pocket Register is a checkbook register app. You log deposits, payments and transfers yourself, and it keeps a running balance, with pending and cleared entries kept apart, like a paper check register. It also shows what's safe to spend before your next payday, built from your own recurring transactions.

Quick facts

  • Made for: people who balance their checkbook by hand, want an exact running balance, and don't want to give an app their bank login.
  • Works on: iPhone, iPad and Mac with Apple silicon, iOS 18.5 or later.
  • Price: free to download. Pro is a one-time purchase ($9.99 in the US; prices vary by country), with Family Sharing for up to 5 people. There is no subscription for the app.
  • Free version: 2 accounts, 300 transactions per 12 months, 5 recurring transactions, reconciling, importing, budgets and the Plan tab. It shows ads.
  • Pro adds: unlimited accounts and transactions, unlimited recurring entries, receipt scanning, bill and low-balance alerts, widgets, iCloud Sync, the Ask feature, color themes, advanced CSV export, and no ads.
  • Privacy: no bank logins. Transactions stay on your device unless you turn on iCloud Sync, which uses your own private iCloud.

How it's different

  • A real register. Running balance, pending vs cleared, and reconciling against your bank statement, the way a paper checkbook works.
  • No bank connection. You enter your own transactions (or type or speak them), so there's nothing to link and nothing to break when a bank changes its login.
  • Easy to switch to. Import your history from other checkbook apps, spreadsheets and banks: CSV, QIF (Quicken, Money and checkbook apps) and OFX/QFX bank downloads. Likely duplicates are flagged before anything is added.
  • Pay once. Pro is a single purchase, not a monthly fee.

What it does

  • Deposits, withdrawals and transfers, with memos and splits.
  • Recurring transactions: weekly, every 2 weeks, twice a month or monthly.
  • Plan tab: projected balance, upcoming bills and debts, and what's safe to spend before payday.
  • Reconcile against your bank statement.
  • Monthly category budgets with progress bars.
  • Receipt photos attached to entries; with Pro, scan a receipt to fill in the store, total and date.
  • Category suggestions as you type, and Quick Fills for repeat entries.
  • Bill reminders and low-balance warnings (Pro).
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets (Pro).
  • Works fully offline.

Questions

Do I have to link my bank?

No. Pocket Register never asks for a bank login. You enter transactions yourself, or import a file you download from your bank.

My old checkbook app stopped working. Can I move my data?

Usually, yes. If your old app or your bank can export a CSV, QIF or OFX/QFX file, Pocket Register can import it. You review everything before it's added. If an export won't import, email it to support@chadpickard.app (remove anything private first) and I'll look at it.

Is it a subscription?

No. The app is free to download, and Pro is a one-time in-app purchase. The optional web companion at pocketregister.app is a separate service with its own subscription; you don't need it to use the app.

Does it sync between iPhone, iPad and Mac?

Yes, with Pro's iCloud Sync, which uses your own private iCloud account. Without it, your data stays on the one device.

Can it show what's safe to spend before payday?

Yes. Set up your paycheck and bills as recurring transactions, and the Plan tab shows your projected balance and what's left until your next payday.

I bought Pro but it isn't showing.

Use Restore Purchases in the app while signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. If that doesn't work, email support@chadpickard.app. More answers are in the full help guide.

Who makes it?

Chad Pickard, an independent developer in the US. Questions and ideas go to support@chadpickard.app, and every email gets a reply.
