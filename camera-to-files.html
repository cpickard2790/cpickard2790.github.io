Camera to Files

Work photos saved straight into the right folder. Files, iCloud Drive, OneDrive or Google Drive.

Camera to Files is an iPhone camera for work photos. You pick a folder once, and every photo, video and scan you take goes straight into it, with a clear file name, instead of piling up in your camera roll. Folders can be in the Files app, iCloud Drive, OneDrive or Google Drive.

  • Privacy policy
  • Contact: support@chadpickard.app

Quick facts

  • Made for: contractors, inspectors, property managers, field techs, adjusters and anyone who takes photos for a job, a client or a project and needs them filed.
  • Works on: iPhone, iOS 18.5 or later.
  • Price: free to download. Pro is a one-time purchase ($7.99 in the US; prices vary by country). There is no subscription.
  • Free version: saving to any folder, OneDrive and Google Drive uploads, date and time stamp, automatic file names, 1 favorite folder, scans up to 3 pages, video up to 30 seconds. It shows ads.
  • Pro adds: unlimited favorite folders, a full photo stamp (GPS, street address, job name, your own text), custom file name patterns, unlimited scans with searchable text and no watermark, 4K/60 video with no time limit, Bulk Rename, batch import from Photos, RAW on supported iPhones, and no ads.
  • Privacy: no account to create. Photos go only to the folder you choose. OneDrive and Google Drive sign-in happens with Microsoft or Google directly.

How it's different

  • The camera saves into the folder. No exporting from Photos afterward and no sorting later. Switch jobs with one tap.
  • Uploads you can trust. Every photo is saved on your iPhone first, then uploaded to OneDrive or Google Drive, retrying until it's done. A dead signal on a job site doesn't lose a shot.
  • Pay once. Many job-photo apps charge monthly per user. Camera to Files Pro is a single purchase.

What it does

  • Photo, video and document scan in one camera.
  • Favorite folders for each job, client or site, switched in one tap.
  • Automatic file names from the date, time and a number; with Pro, your own pattern (job name, place, device and more).
  • Optional Year / Month / Day folders.
  • Date and time stamp on photos; Pro adds GPS, address, job name and your own text.
  • Scan to PDF; Pro adds searchable text (OCR) and color modes.
  • Camera controls: focus and exposure lock, lens switching, timer, grids, JPEG or HEIC, the Camera Control button and volume buttons as shutter.
  • Bulk Rename for files already in a folder (Pro), with undo.
  • Opens straight to the camera from the Action Button or Shortcuts.

Questions

Do my photos also go into the Photos app?

No. Photos save only to the folder you pick, so your camera roll stays personal.

Does it work with OneDrive for work or school?

Yes. It uploads to OneDrive Personal and OneDrive for work or school (Business), and to Google Drive. You can also use any folder the Files app can reach, such as iCloud Drive or a drive provider's folder.

What happens if I have no signal?

The photo is saved on your iPhone first and waits in the Uploads list. It uploads when you're back online and keeps retrying until it's done.

Is it a subscription?

No. The app is free to download, and Pro is a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks everything for good. You can also watch a short ad for a 15-minute Pro preview once a day.

Can I name photos by job or address?

Yes. Free names files by date, time and number. Pro lets you build your own pattern, for example the job name, date and a sequence number.

I bought Pro but it isn't showing.

Use Restore Purchases in the app's settings while signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. If that doesn't work, email support@chadpickard.app.

Who makes it?

Chad Pickard, an independent developer in the US. Questions and ideas go to support@chadpickard.app, and every email gets a reply.
