FieldFraction – Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

FieldFraction does not collect, store, or transmit your data to our servers. The calculations you enter, your tape history, and your settings (such as fraction precision and display preferences) stay on your device.

2. How the App Uses Your Data

FieldFraction uses what you enter only to perform calculations, show results, and keep your tape history. All processing happens on-device. The app works offline.

3. Sharing

If you choose to share your tape, the app passes the text to the iOS share sheet, and you decide where it goes. Nothing is shared automatically.

4. In-App Purchases

FieldFraction offers an optional one-time Pro purchase that unlocks additional tools. Purchases are processed securely through Apple's App Store; the app contacts the App Store only to check and restore your purchase. We do not have access to your payment details.

5. Advertising & Analytics

FieldFraction does not display ads and does not include any advertising or analytics SDKs.

6. Data Security

Your data is stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers for this app, and we do not transmit your data externally. Deleting the app removes its data from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

FieldFraction is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app and on this page with a revised effective date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@chadpickard.app.

FlagHour – Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

FlagHour does not collect, store, or transmit your data to our servers. The app works primarily on your device, and the information you enter — such as repair orders, line items, flagged hours, pay periods, pay plans, job templates, goals, vehicle info, notes, and pay stub photos — remains under your control.

2. How the App Uses Your Data

FlagHour uses the information you enter to track flagged hours, calculate expected pay, manage pay periods, display efficiency metrics, set and track goals, and generate export reports. All processing happens on-device.

3. iCloud Sync

FlagHour offers optional iCloud sync as a Pro feature. When enabled, your data is synced across your devices using Apple's CloudKit framework and your private iCloud account. We do not operate our own sync servers. Your synced data is stored in your personal iCloud storage and is governed by Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy. You can enable or disable iCloud sync at any time in the app's Settings.

4. Camera & Photo Library

FlagHour may use the camera to photograph pay stubs or scan VINs using on-device text recognition. Photos attached to repair orders or pay periods are stored locally on your device, or in your iCloud account if sync is enabled. Photos are not uploaded to any server operated by us.

5. Notifications

If you enable reminders or goal notifications, the app uses Apple notification services to deliver local notifications on your device. Notification scheduling data is stored on-device and is not transmitted to our servers.

6. Live Activities

FlagHour offers optional Live Activities (Pro feature) to display shift status on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. Live Activity data is processed on-device using Apple's ActivityKit framework and is not transmitted to our servers.

7. Widgets

FlagHour provides Home Screen widgets that display summary data such as today's flagged hours, period progress, and expected pay. Widget data is read from a shared on-device App Group container and is not transmitted externally.

8. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

FlagHour offers optional Pro subscriptions (monthly and annual) and a one-time lifetime purchase to unlock additional features such as iCloud sync, Live Activities, and more. Purchases are processed securely through Apple's App Store. We do not have access to your payment details.

9. Advertising

FlagHour does not display ads and does not include any advertising SDKs.

10. Data Security

Your repair order, pay period, and pay plan data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. If iCloud sync is enabled, data is additionally stored in your private iCloud account. We do not operate cloud servers, and we do not transmit your data externally. You can delete all app data at any time from the Settings screen within the app.

11. Children's Privacy

FlagHour is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app and on this page with a revised effective date.

13. Contact Us

Camera to Files – Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

Camera to Files saves photos, videos, and scans directly to folders you choose in the Files app, including iCloud Drive. If you connect Google Drive or OneDrive, it can also upload to a folder you choose there (see section 4). The app itself does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to our servers. There are no accounts with us and no cloud backends operated by us.

2. Device Permissions & How They're Used

  • Camera — capture photos, videos, and document scans.
  • Microphone — record audio with video, if video mode is used.
  • Photo Library — optional, used only when you choose Batch Import to copy selected photos into your target Files folder.
  • Files/Storage — write media to your selected folder using security-scoped bookmarks. The app does not read other folders without your selection.
  • Location — optional. Used only while the camera is open, and only if you turn on Include GPS or a location photo stamp. Location can be saved in the photo's EXIF metadata, in file names, or drawn onto the photo as a stamp. To show a street address on a stamp, the app uses Apple's location service to look up the address for your coordinates; nothing is sent to us. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings.

3. On-Device Processing

Document scanning and OCR text recognition are performed on device using Apple frameworks. We do not upload your scans for processing.

4. Optional Cloud Uploads (Google Drive & OneDrive)

Connecting Google Drive or Microsoft OneDrive is optional. If you connect one, you sign in on Google's or Microsoft's own sign-in screen, and the app uploads the photos, videos and scans you capture directly from your device to the folder you choose in that account. Your files go straight to Google or Microsoft; they never pass through servers operated by us, and we cannot see them.

  • Google Drive access is limited to files the app creates or that you choose to open with it (the drive.file permission). The app does not read the rest of your Drive.
  • OneDrive access lets the app read your basic profile (to show which account is connected) and read and write files so it can list folders and upload to the one you pick.
  • Sign-in tokens are kept in your device's secure Keychain by Google's and Microsoft's sign-in libraries. You can disconnect in the app at any time, and revoke access from your Google or Microsoft account settings.

Camera to Files' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Advertising

When Pro is not active, Camera to Files shows ads from Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads that can temporarily unlock Pro features. Camera to Files does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency), so the app does not give Google your device's advertising identifier. To show ads and measure their performance, Google's ad software may still collect limited data such as an app-specific device identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, the ads you see and tap, and diagnostic data. Google's use of this data is described in Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

6. In-App Purchases & Temporary Pro

  • Permanent Pro — a one-time purchase that removes ads and unlocks Pro features.
  • Rewarded "Try Pro" — watching a short ad grants temporary access to many Pro features. Rewarded access does not permanently unlock additional Favorites; limits may apply once temporary access ends.

All purchases are processed by Apple's App Store. We do not have access to your payment details.

7. Data Security & Retention

Media you create or import is written directly to the folder you choose. Your files remain under your control in the Files app/iCloud Drive. We do not transmit your files to our servers.

8. Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app and on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@chadpickard.app.

Payoff Planner – Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

Payoff Planner does not collect, store, or share personal financial information on our servers. There is no account and no bank linking. The app works on your device, and the financial information you enter--such as debts, balances, bills, income, payment history, and planning settings--remains under your control.

2. How the App Uses Your Data

The app uses the information you enter to build your pay-period plan, roadmap projections, payoff schedule, scenario comparisons, progress history, and payoff recommendations. This processing happens on-device.

3. Interest & Balance Estimates

Payoff Planner may estimate interest in roadmap, schedule, and scenario projections to help forecast payoff timing. However, the app does not automatically apply projected interest to your actual debt balances. Your balances stay based on the values you enter or update, so you remain in control of the numbers shown in the app.

4. Backups & iCloud

You can export a backup file and save it wherever you choose, such as the Files app. With Pro, the app can also save automatic daily backups. If iCloud Drive is turned on for your Apple Account, these backups are saved in the app's folder in your own iCloud Drive (Files > iCloud Drive > Payoff Planner); otherwise they stay on your device. iCloud is provided by Apple under Apple's terms. We do not operate our own cloud servers for backups, and we cannot access your backup files.

5. Advertising

Google's use of this data is described in Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy. Pro removes all ads.

6. In-App Purchases

The app offers an optional one-time in-app purchase (Pro) to remove ads and unlock extra features. Purchases are processed securely through Apple's App Store. We do not have access to your payment details.
