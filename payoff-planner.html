Payoff Planner

A debt payoff plan built around your paycheck. No bank login, no account, no ads, no subscription.

Payoff Planner is an iPhone app for paying off credit cards and loans. You enter your income, bills and debts once. Every pay period it shows how much extra you can safely put toward debt after your bills, minimum payments and living money, which debt to pay first, and the date you'll be debt-free.

Quick facts

  • Made for: people paying down credit cards and loans who want a plan that fits their real paycheck, without connecting a bank.
  • Works on: iPhone, iOS 18.6 or later.
  • Price: free to download, with no ads. Pro is a one-time purchase (prices vary by country). There is no subscription.
  • Free version: the full paycheck plan, every payoff strategy, your debt-free date, interest saved, windfall previews, bill and payday reminders, a 3-month roadmap and manual backups.
  • Pro adds: Be Debt-Free By (pick a date and see what it takes), the full month-by-month payoff schedule as a PDF, Scenario Lab for what-ifs, daily automatic backups, CSV export, the 6- and 12-month roadmap, all-time progress and all card usage targets.
  • Privacy: no account, no bank login and no ads. Your debts and budget are stored on your iPhone, and in your own iCloud Drive if you turn on backups.

How it's different

  • Planned by paycheck, not by month. Payoff Planner follows your real pay schedule (weekly, every 2 weeks, twice a month or monthly). Bills and minimums come first, then your living money and a small cushion. Only what's left goes to debt.
  • No bank connection. Many debt apps ask for your bank login or move your money for you. Payoff Planner never does. You stay in control and pay your debts yourself.
  • Pay once. Many debt payoff apps charge monthly. Payoff Planner is free, and Pro is a single purchase.

What it does

  • The safe extra amount you can put toward debt this paycheck, with a breakdown of income, bills, minimums, living money and cushion.
  • Which debt to pay first, in the order you choose.
  • Your debt-free date and a countdown, plus the interest you save compared with paying only today's minimums.
  • Credit cards, car loans, personal and medical debt, mortgages (with escrow) and student loans (with paused payments).
  • Log payments as you go and watch the debt-free date move closer. Update a balance from your statement at any time.
  • Windfall previews: see what a tax refund or bonus would do before you spend it.
  • Card usage targets: how much to pay before a statement closes to get a card under the next utilization mark.
  • Be Debt-Free By (Pro): pick a date and see the extra you'd need each paycheck to get there.
  • Full payoff schedule (Pro): every month to debt-free, per debt, with payments, interest and balances, saved or printed as a PDF.
  • Scenario Lab (Pro): try changes to income, bills or debts without touching your real plan.
  • Amounts in your own currency.

How the numbers work

  • Extra each paycheck: take-home pay, minus the bills and minimum payments due before your next payday, minus your living reserve and safety cushion.
  • Which debt gets it: one debt at a time, in your strategy's order. When a debt is paid off, its minimum payment is added to the extra for the next one.
  • Interest: each debt's yearly rate is spread over the days between paychecks and added to its balance. A debt with no interest rate entered counts as 0%, so enter the rate from your statement.
  • Interest saved: your plan compared with paying only today's minimums.
  • These are estimates from what you enter. Real statements will differ a little, so update balances now and then. Payoff Planner isn't financial advice.

Questions

Do I have to link my bank account?

No. Payoff Planner never asks for a bank login and never moves money. You enter your debts, bills and income yourself, and log payments when you make them.

Is it a subscription?

No. The app is free to download with no ads, and Pro is a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks everything for good.

What's free and what's Pro?

Free includes the whole paycheck plan, every strategy, your debt-free date, interest saved, reminders and manual backups. Pro adds Be Debt-Free By, the full payoff schedule and PDF, Scenario Lab, daily automatic backups, CSV export, the 6- and 12-month roadmap, all-time progress and all card usage targets.

Should I use snowball or avalanche?

Snowball pays the smallest balance first, so you see debts disappear sooner, which keeps many people going. Avalanche pays the highest interest rate first, which usually costs the least interest overall. Payoff Planner does both, plus a hybrid, highest balance, debt-to-interest ratio, Score Sprint (credit score first) and your own custom order. You can switch any time and compare the debt-free dates.

How much extra do I need to be debt-free by a certain date?

Payoff Planner Pro has Be Debt-Free By: pick a date and it shows the extra you'd need each paycheck, using your real bills, minimums, interest rates and payoff order. Free users see one preview with their own numbers.

Does it handle weekly or every-two-weeks pay?

Yes. Set how often you're paid (weekly, every 2 weeks, twice a month or monthly) and the plan works per paycheck. Minimum payments are planned once a month on their due dates, and the extra goes out each payday.

Does it sync between my iPhone and iPad?

No. There's no account and no live sync. Your data stays on your iPhone. Pro saves a daily automatic backup to your iCloud Drive when it's on, and anyone can export a backup file to move to a new phone.

Does it have ads?

No. Starting with version 1.4.1, Payoff Planner has no ads in the free version or in Pro.

I bought Pro but it isn't showing.

Open More and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. If that doesn't work, email support@chadpickard.app.

Who makes it?
